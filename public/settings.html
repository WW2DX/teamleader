⚠ Reset Logger

Permanently deletes the entire QSO log and resets all stations to a clean state.

The following will be permanently deleted:
  • All logged QSOs on this station's database
  • All operator messages (QST history)
  • Best-hour rate records & DXCC achievement history
  • Goal tracker settings
⚠ This cannot be undone.
